### From Game Worlds to the Real World

**Live deck:** https://charlesb777.github.io/ai_development/ &nbsp;·&nbsp; or just open **`index.html`**

A hands-on workshop that teaches teens (14–17) to build and ship a **real app with AI**.
You already build worlds in Roblox and Minecraft — this is the same builder instinct,
pointed at the real world. By the end, everyone has installed the tools, learned how
pros actually work with AI, and **deployed their own app to the internet.**

## 🧭 What we cover

**0 · Overview** — What AI is, and how an LLM really works: the world's data → broken into
**tokens** (numbers) → pushed through **many layers of math** → out comes your answer.

**1 · Fundamentals** — Thinking in **first principles**; the parts of every app
(**front end · database · user · interface**); and why **math is the language of AI**.

**2 · Coding with AI** — Steering the AI as a **partner, not a vending machine**; the
**four kinds of memory** an AI needs (short-term · long-term · semantic · live / **MCP**);
the **three tools** (Cursor · GitHub · Vercel); and how a **keystroke becomes a live app.**

**3 · Setup** — Install **Cursor**, set up **GitHub**, connect **Vercel** — hands on.

**4 · Vibe-code *well*** — The pro workflow: a **spec is a contract**, the **build loop**
(Brainstorm → Spec → Plan → Build → Review → Test → Ship), teaching the AI from its
**mistakes**, a **reviewer with teeth**, and **proving it works with tests.**

**5 · Deploy & verify** — Ship to **Vercel**, understand **preview vs production**, and
verify your app is live — on your own phone.

**6 · Bonus** — Running **many AI agents at once**, **self-healing builds**, and building
**games** too (full circle back to Roblox).

## 🎯 Two big ideas we come back to
- **From game worlds → the real world.** You didn't wait for someone to make the game — you built it. Now do that for real.
- **DOER vs CHECKER.** Every good build has someone *doing* the work and something *checking* it's right. With AI, you set up both.

## 🛠️ The tools you'll actually use
**Cursor** (AI editor) · **GitHub** (save + undo) · **Vercel** (go live) · **MCP** (live memory)
